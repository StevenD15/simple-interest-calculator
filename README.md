# Simple Interest Calculator

This repository contains a simple interest calculator.

The calculator determines the simple interest based on the principal amount, annual rate of interest, and time period.

## Formula

Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

Where:

- **Principal (P):** Initial amount of money
- **Rate (R):** Annual interest rate
- **Time (T):** Time period in years

## Example

If:

- Principal = 1000
- Rate = 5%
- Time = 2 years

Then:

Simple Interest = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100

Simple Interest = 100

## Usage

The calculator asks the user to enter:

1. Principal amount
2. Annual interest rate
3. Time period in years

It then calculates and displays the simple interest.
